## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add metric-trend
```

The motion variant is `motion-metric-trend`. It installs the plain block too; the tile fades up on
first view and the stacked chart draws along time.

## Usage

```tsx
import { MetricTrend } from '@rectio/blocks/analytics';

<MetricTrend
  label="Monthly recurring revenue"
  points={points}
  previous={mrrFourWeeksAgo}
  comparison="vs 4 weeks ago"
  format="currency"
  currency="EUR"
/>;
```

The latest figure leads, with its change against `previous` (or against the first figure of the
range without it) in a [TrendChip](/docs/components/trend-chip). `better="down"` makes a fall good
news, for churn or costs.

`inline` (the default) is a compact tile with a [Sparkline](/docs/components/sparkline) beside the
figure, for a row of metrics; the line takes the second series colour when the trend goes the wrong
way. `stacked` is a card
with an [AreaChart](/docs/components/area-chart) and its time axis under the figure, an optional
`reference` line such as a target, and the range's high and low in the footer.

## Logic

`metricFigures(points, previous)` gives the latest and first figures, the change, the direction and
the range's high and low; null when the range holds no figure.

## Accessibility

- A region named by its label; the figure and the change are text, and the change says its direction
  and whether it is better in words.
- The sparkline is named in words, such as "MRR, rising from €38,200 to €48,210".
- The stacked chart keeps its summary, keyboard reading and table twin.
