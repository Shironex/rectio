## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx rectio add kanban-board
```

The motion variant is `motion-kanban-board`. It installs the plain block too; cards glide to their
new place.

## Usage

Pass the columns and the cards; a column shows its cards in the order they appear in `cards`. The
board holds no cards itself: every move calls `onMoveCard(cardId, toColumnId, index)`, and
`moveKanbanCard` applies it for you.

A card moves three ways: drag it, open its Move menu (to any column, up, down), or focus it and
press Alt with an arrow key. A column with a `limit` shows its count against it and says in words
when it runs over. Due dates turn into "Overdue" and "Due today" as the day comes. On narrow
widths the columns scroll sideways and snap.

## Logic

Moves are plain functions, tested on their own: `moveKanbanCard`, `kanbanStep`,
`kanbanColumnCards`, `kanbanPositionOf`, `kanbanOverLimit` and `kanbanDueState`.

## Accessibility

- Every card is one focus stop, described by the hint that explains Alt and the arrow keys.
- Each move is read out in a polite live region, with the column and the new position, and focus
  stays on the card.
- The Move menu does everything a drag can, so no move needs a pointer.
- Limits and due dates are written in words with an icon, never by colour alone.
- Works in both themes, light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity and renders the plain block under reduced motion.
