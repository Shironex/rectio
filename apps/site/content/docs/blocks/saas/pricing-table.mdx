## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add pricing-table
```

The motion variant is `motion-pricing-table`. It installs the plain block too.

## Usage

Pass two to four plans, cheapest first, with a monthly price and, where you offer one, a yearly
price for the whole year. Prices show per month on both cycles, with the yearly total and the saving
spelled out under them; the cycle switch names the best saving. One plan can be `recommended`; the
plan someone is on, `currentPlanId`, says "You are on this plan" in place of a button. Plans with a `null`
price read Custom, and a price of 0 reads Free. Features below compare every plan, with a check or a
dash and the words for each; on a narrow block the comparison becomes one section per plan. Use `bare` on a landing page to drop the panel frame.

## Logic

`pricingMonthlyPrice`, `pricingYearlySaving`, `pricingBestSaving` and `groupPricingFeatures` are
exported and tested on their own; monthly equivalents round to the currency's minor unit.

## Accessibility

- The cycle switch is a labelled group of pressed buttons; each plan is a list item named for itself.
- Every plan button names its plan; check and dash marks read Included and Not included.
- The comparison is a real table that scrolls sideways on a narrow block with row names kept in view.
- Works in light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant renders the plain block under reduced motion.
