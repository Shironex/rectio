## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add funnel-card
```

The motion variant is `motion-funnel-card`; the stages grow one after another on first view.

## Usage

```tsx
import { FunnelCard } from '@rectio/blocks/analytics';

<FunnelCard
  title="Trial to paid"
  description="Last 30 days"
  stages={stages}
  previous={stagesTheMonthBefore}
  comparison="vs the 30 days before"
/>;
```

A [ChartCard](/docs/components/chart-card) over a [FunnelChart](/docs/components/funnel-chart). The
headline is the share of the first stage that reached the last, and with `previous` its change in
percentage points. A line above the chart names the step that loses the largest share of the stage
before it.

`variant` sets the funnel's orientation: `horizontal` (the default) for long stage names and phones,
`vertical` for a wide card with a few stages.

## Logic

`funnelInsight(stages, previous)` gives the conversion, the previous conversion, the change in points
and the biggest drop.

## Accessibility

- A region named by its title; the conversion, its change and the biggest drop are text.
- The funnel lists every stage with its figure, share and step in words, with a summary and a table
  twin.
