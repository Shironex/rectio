## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add comment-thread
```

The motion variant is `motion-comment-thread`. It installs the plain block too; comments arrive and
leave in place.

## Usage

Pass the comments and the person writing. Comments show oldest first with their replies one level
deep; a reply to a reply joins the same thread. The writer gets Edit and Delete on their own
comments, and delete asks first; pass `canModerate` to let the viewer delete anyone's comment (editing
stays with the author). After a delete, focus moves to the next comment, else the previous one, else
the block heading. `@mentions` are picked out. The composer shows its send shortcut as keys and sends
with Ctrl or Cmd and Enter, and refuses an empty comment with a reason.

Pass `resolved` to close the discussion: a banner says who resolved it and when, the composer and
Reply buttons give way, and `onReopen` adds a Reopen button.

## Logic

Threading and checks are exported and tested on their own: `commentTree`, `commentParticipants`,
`commentBodyError` and `commentParts`.

## Accessibility

- Each comment is an article named by its author and time; the full date is on the time's title.
- Every composer has a label, and its error is tied to the field.
- Reply, Edit and Delete name the comment they act on; the delete dialog quotes it.
- Works in light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity and renders the plain block under reduced motion.
