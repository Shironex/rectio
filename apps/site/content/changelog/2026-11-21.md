## Kit

- The notifications popover keeps its filter on the popup surface, shows its scrollbar at rest with four whole rows in view, and quiets the bodies of read notifications.
- Segmented options in blocks never wrap: a narrow block lays them out in two columns, and the chosen option is a raised chip like the kit's segmented controls.
- Button infers `nativeButton` from `render`, so a link drawn as a button keeps its link role and gets no `type`.
- Sheet takes a `size` of `sm`, `md` or `lg` on the side edges, with the widths from tokens.
- Meter draws gauge ticks across the track and places bar ticks on the right side in right to left layouts.
- ScrollArea takes `scrollbar="always"` to keep its scrollbar visible while the content overflows.
- Billing names a single allowance unit in the singular.
- Breadcrumb shows the name of its overflow menu on hover as well.
- Block panels keep their actions beside the title while there is room, and the billing plan actions stay under their plan.

## Templates

- The help desk ticket sheets and the expense sheet use the new Sheet sizes.
- The template sample data uses English names and places.

## Docs and site

- Every demo, docs example and message example is in English; the Polish locale demo is gone.
