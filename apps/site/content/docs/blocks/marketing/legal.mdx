## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add legal
```

This block has no motion variant: nothing on it moves.

The marketing blocks share a small core (`marketing-core`) that `add` installs for you.

A privacy policy, terms of service or cookie policy page: title, last updated date, a table of
contents that follows the reader on wide screens and folds into a disclosure on narrow ones, and the
text in prose styles.

## Legal text is a template

The exported `PRIVACY_POLICY_TEMPLATE`, `TERMS_TEMPLATE` and `COOKIE_POLICY_TEMPLATE` are
placeholders, not legal text. Every bracketed value is yours to fill and every section must be
rewritten for your business, your users and the law that applies to you, ideally with a lawyer.
Replace with your own legal text: this is not legal advice. While `template` is on, the page shows
a notice saying so. Leave it on until your own text is in.

## Usage

Turn a template into sections with `templateSections(template)`, or write your own `LegalSection`
list: a title and content as plain HTML elements.

## Accessibility

- The table of contents is a labelled navigation and marks the section in view with `aria-current="location"`.
- On narrow screens it folds into a disclosure that is closed by default.
- Headings get unique ids made from their titles, so every section can be linked.
