## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add donut-chart
```

## Usage

```tsx
import { DonutChart } from '@/components/ui/donut-chart';

<DonutChart
  data={[
    { id: 'payroll', label: 'Payroll', value: 184200 },
    { id: 'software', label: 'Software', value: 42800 },
  ]}
  format="currency"
  currency="EUR"
/>;
```

The ring is a picture of the list beside it: every part keeps its name, figure and share in the
list, so nothing is read from angles alone. Past `maxSlices` (four by default) the smallest parts fold
into one Other part in the reference colour, and the table names each part inside it. Without a colour
on every part, at most three parts and Other are drawn, so no colour repeats. Zero and negative figures
are left out, since they cannot be a share of a whole.

The middle shows the total, written in full when it fits and short when it does not; pass
`centerValue` for your own figure, or `null` when the card around it already shows the total. In a
narrow column the list moves under the ring.

The motion variant settles the parts in around the ring on first view.

## Accessibility

- A figure with a summary read first: the total and every part with its share.
- The list beside the ring names every part with its figure and share; the figure in the middle is
  hidden from screen readers, since the summary says it.
- The table twin adds the total row, shown on request with **Show table**.
- Other is drawn in the reference colour, apart from the three series for every common colour vision
  type, and a hairline of the surface separates neighbours.
