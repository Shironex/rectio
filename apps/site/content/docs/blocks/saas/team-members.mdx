## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add team-members
```

The motion variant is `motion-team-members`. It installs the plain block too; members and invites
arrive and leave in place.

## Usage

Pass the members, the pending invites and the role of the person looking. The block offers only
what that role may do: owners hand out any role, admins up to admin, members and viewers see the
list. Nobody can change themselves or remove the last owner. Removing someone asks first, in a
dialog that names them. After you remove a member or revoke an invite, focus moves to the next row, else
the previous one, else the heading.

The invite form takes pasted addresses separated by commas, spaces or new lines, sets aside the
ones already on the team, and refuses more invites than the plan has seats.

## Logic

The role rules are exported and tested on their own, so a server can enforce the same ones:
`canManage`, `assignableRoles`, `parseInviteEmails`, `inviteError`, `seatUsage` and `sortMembers`.

## Accessibility

- Every role select, menu and button names the person or the address it acts on.
- The row menu works with arrow keys, Enter and Escape, and returns focus to its button.
- Invite errors are tied to the field and say which addresses to check.
- Works in light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity and renders the plain block under reduced motion.
