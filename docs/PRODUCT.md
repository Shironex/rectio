# rectio

register: product

## Product purpose

rectio is a free, open source (MIT) React design library for finance and back-office software:
expense tools, ledgers, invoicing, approvals, treasury dashboards, and the SaaS, marketing and docs
screens around them. It ships tokens, components, blocks and whole templates that product teams
theme to their brand and compose into dense, trustworthy working screens.

## Users

- Product engineers at fintech and B2B SaaS companies who build internal and customer-facing
  finance screens and need accessible components that look finished without a designer on hand.
- The people who use those screens: finance operations staff, accountants, approvers. They work
  long sessions, scan columns of figures, enter amounts precisely and cannot afford ambiguity
  about signs, currencies or validation.

## Brand and tone

Calm, exact, unshowy. The interface should feel like good stationery and a well-kept ledger:
quiet surfaces, confident type, figures that line up. Personality comes from typography and
restraint, not decoration.

Two shipped themes show the range: `default` (warm neutral, cobalt accent, IBM Plex) and
`ledger` (green-black ink, citron accent, Source Serif numerals, sharper corners).

## Anti-references

- Navy and gold "bank" palettes; neon-on-black crypto dashboards.
- SaaS hero metrics, gradient accents, glass cards, decorative illustrations.
- A stock component kit look shipped unchanged.

## Principles

1. Numbers first: tabular figures, true minus signs, currency always explicit.
2. Never colour alone: signs, icons and text carry meaning; colour reinforces.
3. Keyboard complete: every control reachable and operable without a pointer.
4. Tokens only: components never hardcode colour, radius, type, shadow or motion.
