## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add billing
```

The motion variant is `motion-billing`. It installs the plain block too; invoice rows arrive in place.

## Usage

Pass the plan, the usage this period, the card on file and the invoices, all in one currency. The
plan tile says what it costs and when it renews; usage meters say in words how much is left, stay calm at the allowance exactly,
warn from 90 per cent and turn red past it. The figure beside each label is a percentage; the "X of Y"
text sits in the description and is the meter's spoken value. The bar is accent while there is room,
warning near the limit and danger over it. A card that expires this month or next says so, and a
past due plan opens with an alert naming the amount owed. Every action is a callback: the block
never moves money itself.

## Logic

`billingUsageLevel`, `billingUsageNote`, `billingCardState`, `billingDaysLeft` and
`billingInvoiceTotals` are exported and tested on their own. Totals add in minor units, so paid and
owed never drift by a cent.

## Accessibility

- Usage meters are real meters: the "X of Y" text is their spoken value, and the level is in words, not only in colour.
- Each download button names its invoice; void amounts are struck through and say Void.
- A past due plan is announced as an alert.
- Works in both themes, light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity and renders the plain block under reduced motion.
