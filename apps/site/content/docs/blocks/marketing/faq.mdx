## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add faq
```

The motion variant is `motion-faq`. It installs the plain block too and changes only how the
block enters.

The marketing blocks share a small core (`marketing-core`) that `add` installs for you.

Frequently asked questions. The accordion opens one answer at a time beside the heading; the
numbered list shows every answer at once in two columns, for five or six questions everyone should
read.

## Usage

Answers are nodes, so they can hold links. Set `defaultOpen` to the ids of the answers open at first,
such as the most asked question. Use `aside` for a link to support under the heading.

## Accessibility

- Closed answers stay findable with find in page.
- The numbered list shows every answer at once, so nothing is hidden from a reader who skips the toggles.
