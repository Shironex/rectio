Blocks are whole pieces of a finance screen, built only from rectio components and tokens: the
kind of composition every expense, ledger or approval tool ends up writing by hand.

<Callout>
  Blocks are in progress. This section lists each block with a live preview as it ships.
</Callout>

Until then, every block's parts are documented under Components, starting with
[DataTable](/docs/components/data-table) and [Money](/docs/components/money).
