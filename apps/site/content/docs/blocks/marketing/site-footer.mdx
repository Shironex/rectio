## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add site-footer
```

This block has no motion variant: nothing on it moves.

The marketing blocks share a small core (`marketing-core`) that `add` installs for you.

The bottom of every page: brand and tagline, link columns, social links and the small print.

## Usage

`layout="columns"` is a full site map; `minimal` folds everything into one or two rows for a
single-page site. Use `extra` for a compact newsletter signup or a status badge. Add a "Cookie
settings" link to `legal` that opens the cookie preferences.

## Accessibility

- The block is a footer landmark with a Footer navigation and a Legal navigation.
- Social links are icon-only, so each keeps its label as an accessible name.
- Links render through the kit's `Anchor`, so a [`LinkProvider`](/docs/components/link) sends them
  through your router.
