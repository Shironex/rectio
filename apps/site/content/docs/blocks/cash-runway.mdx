## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx rectio add cash-runway
```

The motion variant is `motion-cash-runway`. It installs the plain block too and changes only how
the block enters.

## Usage

Pass the monthly history of cash in and out and the balance at the end of its last month. The block
averages the net burn over the last 3, 6 or 12 months (a switch offers the windows the history
fills), divides the balance by it for the runway, and says in words the month cash runs out. When
more comes in than goes out it says cash is not burning instead of a number.

The chart shows month-end balances: the history solid, worked back from today's balance, then the
projection dashed toward the zero line. `planned` adds known one-offs to the projection, such as a
funding round or an annual licence; `threshold` draws a minimum cash line and names the month the
balance drops below it. A runway under `alertMonths` (6 by default) is emphasised.

```tsx
<CashRunway
  history={history}
  balance={412_600}
  currency="EUR"
  planned={[{ month: new Date(2026, 5, 1), amount: -18_000, label: 'Annual insurance' }]}
  threshold={100_000}
/>
```

## Messages

Every word comes from `messages`; the English defaults are `CASH_RUNWAY_MESSAGES`, typed as
`CashRunwayMessages`. Messages that name a month get a `MonthWords` with the formatted text and the
date itself, so a language that needs another case of the month's name can build it:

```tsx
<CashRunway
  {...props}
  messages={{
    runwayMonths: (months, count) =>
      `${months} ${plural(count, { one: 'miesiąc', few: 'miesiące', many: 'miesięcy', other: 'miesiąca' }, 'pl-PL')}`,
  }}
/>
```

## Logic

`averageBurn(history, currency, window)` and `cashRunway(history, balance, currency, options)` are
exported and tested on their own: burn, runway in months, the zero-cash and threshold months and
the balance series, every sum in minor units.

## Accessibility

- The chart has a summary read before the plot and a table of the same balances, with planned changes named.
- Arrow keys on the chart step through the months; the tooltip follows.
- The runway and the month cash runs out are said in words, not left to the line.
- Works in both themes, light and dark, at compact density and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity, plays once on first view and renders the plain block under reduced motion.
