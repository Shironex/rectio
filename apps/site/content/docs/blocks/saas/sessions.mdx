## Install

```sh
pnpm dlx @rectio/cli add sessions
```

The motion variant is `motion-sessions`. It installs the plain block too; a signed-out session leaves
and the rows below close the gap.

## Usage

Pass the account's sessions with the one this page runs in marked `current`. It comes first with a
This device badge and no sign out button: that session ends by signing out. The others follow, most
recently active first, each with its device, browser and system, where it was last seen and its IP
address when you have them, and "Active now" for anything seen in the last five minutes.

`onRevoke` signs one session out at once, without a dialog: signing in again is cheap, and the
button shows progress for any id you list in `revoking` until the session leaves the list.
`onRevokeOthers` adds Sign out other sessions, which asks first. Pass `error` to say a sign out
failed, and `passwordHref` to link the note under the list to your password page.

## Logic

Exported and tested on their own: `sortSessions`, `isActiveNow`, `sessionName` and `otherSessions`.

## Accessibility

- The sessions are a labelled list; each sign out button names the session it ends.
- The device kind is written for screen readers, not only drawn as an icon.
- After a sign out, focus moves to the next session, else the previous one, never to the page.
- The confirm dialog names how many sessions it ends; a failed sign out is an alert.
- Works in light and dark and down to phone width with 44px touch targets.
- The motion variant moves only transform and opacity and renders the plain block under reduced
  motion.
